Privacy Policy for Turbo Pong Clash

At Mungalabs, we prioritze your privacy. This policy applies to Turbo Pong Clash, a fast-paced arcade game.

1. Local Data

Your high scores, gold, and rank are stored 100% locally via SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Uses Advertising IDs to show relevant content.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Hardens game stability and tracks engagement anonymously.

3. Safety & Privacy

We comply with COPPA and the Play Families Policy. We do not collect personal data from children under 13.
