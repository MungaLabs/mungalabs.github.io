Privacy Policy for Aura Zenith

At Mungalabs, we believe that gaming should be accessible and private. This Privacy Policy applies to the Aura Zenith application, a fast-paced arcade game designed to test your reflexes and speed.

1. Local Data Management

Your gaming experience is managed locally on your device and via encrypted cloud synchronization (Firebase). We store:

  • High Scores: Your best performance metrics.
  • Virtual Currency: Balances earned through active gameplay.
  • Progress: Unlocked skins, power-up levels, and Prestige Ranks.

2. Analytics & Ads

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements. AdMob may access your device’s Advertising ID.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to understand general engagement and maintain app stability. No personal identifiers are used.

3. Data Security

We take Data Safety seriously. All communication between the app and third-party services is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS/TLS protocols.
