Privacy Policy for Aura Zenith

At Mungalabs, we believe that gaming should be accessible and private. This Privacy Policy applies to the Aura Zenith application, a fast-paced arcade game designed to test your reflexes and speed.

1. Data Management & Synchronization

Aura Zenith is designed to provide a "Bulletproof" gaming experience. We manage your progress using both local storage (SharedPreferences) and secure Cloud Synchronization (Firebase Firestore). We store:

  • High Scores & Speed Records: To track your global standing.
  • Virtual Currency (Gold): Earned through active gameplay.
  • Shop Progress: Unlocked skins, power-up levels, and Prestige Ranks.
  • Sync Data: Anonymized account identifiers used solely to restore your progress if you change devices.

2. Professional Integration & Advertising

To provide a high-quality experience and maintain development, we integrate industry-standard third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may access your device’s Advertising ID to provide relevant, family-friendly content. You can manage or reset this ID in your Android device settings.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to understand general user engagement and identify game-balancing needs. No personal identifiers are used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Sends anonymized crash reports to help us maintain our 100% stability standard.

3. Children’s Privacy & Safety (COPPA Compliance)

Aura Zenith is designed for a general audience and strictly complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

  • We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
  • No user accounts or social logins are required, ensuring total anonymity for younger users.
  • Advertisements are filtered to ensure they follow standard "Family-Friendly" guidelines.

4. Data Security

We take Data Safety seriously. All communication between the app and third-party services (Firebase/AdMob) is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS/TLS protocols. Your cloud data is protected by Google Cloud's professional security infrastructure.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

Your privacy is our priority. You have full control over your data:

  • Local Data: You can clear your game progress by clearing the application cache/data in your Android system settings.
  • Cloud Data: To request a permanent deletion of your cloud-synced progress, you can contact us at the email provided below with your device ID (found in settings).
  • Uninstallation: Deleting the app will stop all further data collection.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in game features or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page.
